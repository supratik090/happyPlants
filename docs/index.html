Privacy Policy for Metro Greens

Application: Metro Greens • Developer / Entity: Supratik De • Effective Date: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Metro Greens ("the App"), developed by Supratik De ("we", "us", or "our"), handles your information when you use our mobile application available on the Google Play Store.

We value your privacy. Metro Greens is designed as a personal urban plant care companion and botanical diagnostic tool. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data.

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

Metro Greens requests and uses only the minimal device features necessary to provide core plant care functionality:

  • Location Data (Coarse & Fine Location): Used on-demand to fetch real-time local temperature, humidity, and Air Quality Index (AQI) via the Open-Meteo API. This allows the app to calculate Vapor Pressure Deficit (VPD) and deliver accurate, climate-tailored watering and placement recommendations. Your location is never tracked in the background, never logged to any remote server, and never shared.
  • Camera & Photos: Used solely when you actively take or select a photo of a plant or leaf for identification and disease diagnosis, and for the ambient camera light meter feature. Photos submitted for botanical identification are processed securely via the PlantNet API or stored strictly locally on your device for your personal garden album.
  • Local Garden & Care Logs: Information you enter about your rooms, plants, watering schedules, and notes is stored locally on your device in a private SQLite database.
  • Notifications: Used solely to deliver scheduled morning reminders for plants that require watering, fertilising, or attention. You can disable notifications anytime in the app or system settings.
Advertising & Tracking Policy:
Metro Greens does not display third-party advertisements and does not use or collect the Google Advertising ID (AD_ID). Tracking cookies and advertising tracking SDKs are explicitly disabled.

2. Third-Party Services

The app interacts with trusted external services strictly to provide core features:

  • Open-Meteo: For retrieving localized weather and environmental parameters. No user credentials or personal identifiers are sent.
  • PlantNet API: For plant identification and disease recognition from user-submitted plant photos.
  • Google Play Services & Firebase: For basic application integrity and anonymous crash diagnostics to ensure app stability across Android versions.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your garden records and plant care schedules are stored locally on your device, you have complete control over your data. You can delete individual plants, photos, or logs at any time from within the app. Clearing the app storage or uninstalling the app permanently removes all local data from your device.

4. Children's Privacy

Metro Greens is suitable for general audiences of all ages and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe any personal information has been inadvertently collected, please contact us immediately.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Effective Date".
