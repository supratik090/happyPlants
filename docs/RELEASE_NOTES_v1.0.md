## Detailed Highlights & Changelog

### 1. Expanded Botanical Encyclopaedia (220 Species)
* **Unified Offline Catalogue (`indian_v1.json`)**: 220 indoor, balcony, succulent, fern, herb, flowering, and edible species packaged directly into the app for 100% offline access.
* **Vernacular Nursery Names**: Authentic local names across 6 regional languages (Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu).
* **Tiered Popularity Scoring**: Balanced rankings prioritising popular home plants.
* **Optimised Library Browsing**: Fast incremental rendering with visual loading indicators and zero UI freeze.

### 2. Environmental Intelligence & Light Meter
* **Dual-Mode Light Meter**: Supports both built-in hardware ambient light sensors and camera-based lux reading.
* **Camera Auto-Shutoff**: Automatically locks lux readings and releases camera hardware when finished.
* **Climate Analytics**: Real-time Vapor Pressure Deficit (VPD), Daily Light Integral (DLI), and 7-day cumulative microclimate tracking.
* **Space Recommendations**: Matched plant recommendations tailored to measured room lighting, humidity, and temperature.

### 3. Smart Garden Care & Reminders
* **Adaptive Seasonal Scheduling**: Separate watering schedules optimised for hot/dry months versus monsoon and winter periods.
* **Holistic Plant Maintenance**: Scheduled reminders for fertilising, organic manure, topsoil refreshment, and repotting.
* **Daily Duty Notifications**: Battery-friendly local alerts notifying you of scheduled morning care tasks.

### 4. Interactive Learning & Diagnosis
* **AI Plant & Disease Identification**: Pl@ntNet-powered identification and instant leaf disease assessment.
* **Daily Botanical Quiz & Trivia**: Quick interactive quizzes to test and expand your urban gardening knowledge.

### 5. Visual Identity & Polish
* **Warm Editorial Theme**: Refined colour palette (`Forest`, `Moss`, `Sand`, `SurfaceWarm`, `PaperDark`).
* **Modern Launch Screen**: Brand-new Metro Greens emblem with smooth startup animations.
